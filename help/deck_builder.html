Deck Builder

Switch the left panel to Builder mode to search for individual cards and assemble your own deck. Builder mode is accessed by clicking the Builder toggle in the left panel header when a deck is loaded.

Controls

ControlDescription
Search box Type a card name or keyword. Results appear in the list below as you type. The card database must be downloaded before searching (see Settings → Update Card Database).
Result list Click a result to preview it in the Card Inspector.
Add to Main Adds one copy of the selected card to the Mainboard. With a result selected in the list, you can also press 1, 2, 3 or 4 to add that many copies to the Mainboard.
Add to Side Adds one copy of the selected card to the Sideboard. With a result selected in the list, you can also press Shift+1, Shift+2, Shift+3 or Shift+4 to add that many copies to the Sideboard.
Mana Keyboard Opens the floating mana-symbol keyboard. Click a symbol to insert it into the search field, which allows searching by mana cost.
Back to Research Returns the left panel to Metagame Research mode.

Result list shortcuts

Double-clicking a result adds one copy of that card to the active deck zone — the mainboard or sideboard, whichever you last worked in.

When a card is selected in the result list, these keys add copies without leaving the keyboard:

KeyAction
Double-clickAdd one copy to the active zone.
+Add one copy to the active zone.
1 / 2 / 3 / 4 Add 1, 2, 3 or 4 copies to the Mainboard.
Shift+1 / Shift+2 / Shift+3 / Shift+4 Add 1, 2, 3 or 4 copies to the Sideboard.

Editing counts

Once a card is in the deck, use the + / − buttons in the Deck Workspace card rows to adjust counts, or double-click a card there to take one copy out. Right-clicking a row provides additional options such as removing the card entirely.

When the search is locked

While you are recording a Sideboard Guide, this panel is greyed out and adds nothing. That walk records each matchup as the difference between the board you leave on screen and your base 75, so a card pulled in from the search would be stored as a card to bring in that is not in your deck at all. Finish or cancel the recording to use the search again.

Radar analysis

Click Radar (available in Builder mode) to open the radar dialog, which shows how frequently cards appear across archetypes in the current format. This helps identify format-staple cards when building.

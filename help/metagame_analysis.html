Metagame Analysis

The Metagame Analysis window gives a top-level breakdown of a competitive format. Open it with Tools ▸ Metagame Analysis on the menu bar.

It chooses its own format rather than following the main window: the Format dropdown along the top offers Modern, Standard, Pioneer, Legacy, Vintage and Pauper, and the window opens on Modern whatever the main window is showing. Beside it, Time window sets how many days are pooled into the share figures (1 to 7) and Day moves that window back in time (up to 30 days ago), so you can look at an earlier stretch of the format or compare one against another.

Contents

  • Archetype share chart — a sorted bar chart of how much of the metagame each archetype represents, based on MTGGoldfish data. The twelve largest get a bar each and everything below them is pooled into Other archetypes.
  • Metagame Changes — each archetype’s share now against its share in the preceding period, with an up or down arrow. It needs data from both periods, and says so when it has none to compare.

Share is measured by how many decks of each archetype were published, not by how they did; the window reports no win rates.

Refreshing data

Data is fetched from MTGGoldfish and cached locally for a day. Click Refresh Data at the top of the window to pull it again.
